ColumnDescription

year_onlineExisting or target year
investment_usdAnnounced capital investment in dollars, where an operator publishes $ but not MW (data centers)
capacity_estimatedtrue when MW is derived from a stated $ or square-footage figure rather than an operator-published power number
energy_sourceDedicated or co-located generation where disclosed (e.g. mixed, gas, solar)
sourcePrimary public source URL

Change-log rows (subscribers) add kind (added / removed / changed), field, from, to, and the refresh period.

Questions people ask

The battery data comes from a free federal file. Why pay?

You're not paying for the records — you're paying to never think about them again. EIA-860M is a multi-sheet spreadsheet whose layout shifts, that needs de-duplication against real-world sites, and that says nothing about what changed since last month. We do that on the 26th, every month, and hand you a clean file and a diff. Data centers have no federal registry at all; that layer is hand-verified from operator disclosures with a source on every row.

What's the coverage floor?

Battery records cover utility-scale systems of 1 MW and above that report to the federal inventory. Behind-the-meter, residential, and small commercial storage are out of scope. Data center records focus on hyperscale, major colocation, and AI training campuses.

Can I put this in a product my customers see?

Not on Snapshot or Always Current — those are single-user licenses for your own analysis and internal tools. Region, Full, and Embedded tiers include a redistribution license. Because our BESS source is public domain, there are no upstream restrictions to pass through to you.

How does access work for Always Current?

After checkout you land on a personal access page. Bookmark it — it's your download link every month, and it stays live while your subscription is active. Manage or cancel billing from the same page. If you lose the link, email hello@usenergymap.com from your purchase address.

Can I get an invoice, PO, or W-9?

Yes for the annual tiers — that's how they're billed. Self-serve tiers get a Stripe receipt automatically; ask if you need more.
