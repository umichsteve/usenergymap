What's on the map

Two technologies, one grid story

Data centers are driving record electricity demand. Battery storage is racing to balance it. We map both side by side so you can see how — and where — they're colliding.

▣

Data centers

Hyperscale and colocation campuses from AWS, Google, Microsoft, Meta, Apple, Equinix, Digital Realty and more — hand-curated from operator announcements and industry reporting.

▮

Battery storage (BESS)

Utility-scale battery projects auto-ingested every month from the federal EIA-860M generator inventory — power (MW) and, where reported, energy (MWh).

◆

Status & timeline

Every project is tagged operating, under construction, or planned — with a target year — so you can filter the map to today's grid or tomorrow's pipeline.

◈

Grid context

Toggle ISO/RTO regions (ERCOT, CAISO, PJM, MISO, SPP, NYISO, ISO-NE) to see where the buildout concentrates inside each power market.

The map

Filter, search, and drill in

Markers are colored by type, ringed by status, and sized by capacity. Cluster on zoom-out; click any project for operator, capacity, status, and a link to the primary source.

Smart filters

Type, status, state, operator, minimum MW, year-online range, and free-text search — all live, all instant.

Synced project list

A sortable sidebar mirrors your filters. Click a row to fly the map there and open its popup.

Deep links

Every project has a shareable URL — open the map straight to a single site with /map#project-id.
