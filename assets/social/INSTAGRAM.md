## Ongoing content pillars

1. **Map moments** — screenshots/clips of interesting clusters or new entries.
2. **The numbers** — single-stat brand cards (monthly EIA refresh = built-in cadence).
3. **Explainers** — what's a BESS? what's an ISO/RTO? why is demand spiking?
4. **Buildout news** — react to major project announcements, then point to the map.

All graphics should reuse the brand: midnight background, blue→cyan gradient mark, white bolt, blue = data center, green = battery.
