What this map covers

There is no federal registry of data centers, so this layer is curated by hand from operator announcements, utility and permitting filings, and trade reporting, with the source linked on each row. It covers hyperscale campuses, AI training sites and the major colocation portfolios. It does not yet list every smaller colocation or enterprise facility. Coordinates are the exact site where it is public and the city or county centroid where it is not. The methodology has the full definitions, and you can suggest a project we are missing.
